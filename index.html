Field Test Guide

Use it in real work—not as a demo.

  1. Before a building visit: open a school, read Executive Focus, then use 1-Page Brief.
  2. During a conversation: switch to Meeting Mode and capture only what changed or surfaced.
  3. Between meetings: ask “What needs my attention?” and “What can wait?”
  4. When comparing schools: confirm the questions actually differ based on the evidence.
  5. At the end of the day: open Executive Memory and see whether decisions/follow-ups are easy to recover.

Device test: repeat at least one school workflow on phone and laptop/desktop. Check text size, tap targets, scrolling, Home, Meeting Mode, memory persistence, and 1-Page Brief.
